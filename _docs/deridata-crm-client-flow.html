How We Price Bonds Using DeriData

A simple guide to our CRM bond pricing process

Prepared for client presentation · MeraDhan · Bond pricing in CRM Autofill

What is this about?

When our operations team prices a bond in CRM, we use DeriData — a trusted market calculator — to get accurate price, interest, and settlement amounts.

We do this in two clear steps, so the final numbers always match whether the bond is in a normal interest period or a special “shut” (ex-interest) period.

The journey in plain words

1

Team enters basic details

Bond ID (ISIN), settlement date, quantity, and either a target yield or a clean price.

↓
2

First DeriData call — understand the calendar

We ask DeriData for the bond’s interest schedule and record date, so we know where the settlement date sits in the coupon cycle.

↓
3

We decide: normal period or shut period?

Using those dates, we check if the buyer is settling during the shut window (after the record date, before the next coupon). This decides how accrued interest should be treated.

↓
4

Second DeriData call — get the final money amounts

We call DeriData again with the correct shut / normal setting, and receive the final price, accrued interest, and settlement total.

↓
5

Team reviews and saves

Values appear in CRM Autofill. After Accept, they are saved on the bond and used for customer orders and PDFs (scaled by quantity) — without calling DeriData again at checkout.

Step by step — what each DeriData call does

Call 1 · Calendar check

Ask DeriData: “What are the important dates for this bond?”

We send the bond identity, settlement date, and quantity. DeriData returns the interest payment schedule and the record date.

What we learn: Last coupon date · Next coupon date · Record date · Whether settlement falls in the shut window · How many accrual days apply
Our decision · In between the two calls

Decide shut period vs normal period

Shut period means: settlement is on or after the record date, and before the next coupon payment. In this window, accrued interest is handled differently (often shown as a deduction / negative accrual days toward the next coupon).

Normal period means: settlement is before the record date. Accrued interest builds up from the last coupon to the settlement date.

Why this matters: If we skip this check and price with the wrong setting, accrued interest and the final amount payable can be wrong.
Call 2 · Final pricing

Ask DeriData: “Given the correct period, what should the buyer pay?”

We send the same bond details again, plus our shut / normal decision. DeriData returns the full pricing breakdown.

What we get back: Clean price · Yield · Principal · Accrued interest (in rupees) · Total consideration · Stamp duty · Final settlement amount

What the customer / ops team sees as results

Value In simple terms
Clean price The bond price excluding accrued interest (as a % of face value)
Yield The expected return if held to maturity (or derived from the clean price)
Principal Clean price applied to face value × quantity
Accrued interest Interest earned up to settlement (or adjusted in shut period) — in rupees
Total consideration Principal + accrued interest
Stamp duty Regulatory charge on the trade
Settlement amount Final amount: consideration + stamp duty

Why two calls instead of one?

If we only called once

  • We might price with the wrong shut / normal setting
  • Accrued interest could be incorrect
  • Settlement amount shown to the customer could be wrong

With our two-step approach

  • First call learns the real dates from DeriData
  • We set shut / normal correctly
  • Second call returns money amounts that match that decision
Speed note: If the first call already used the same shut setting we need for pricing, we reuse that result and skip the second call — so the team is not kept waiting.

Where DeriData is used — and where it is not

Place Uses DeriData? What happens
CRM Autofill (ops team) Yes — two-step flow Live calculation, then team Accepts and saves
Customer place-order page No Uses the saved CRM values, scaled by the order quantity
Order / draft PDF No Uses the same saved values shown for that order

Takeaway for stakeholders

  • Accurate: Pricing respects shut vs normal interest periods.
  • Consistent: What ops Accepts in CRM is what customers see at order time.
  • Clear ownership: DeriData is used only in CRM autofill; checkout does not depend on a live external call.
  • Transparent: Final amounts (price, interest, stamp duty, settlement) come from DeriData’s calculator after our period check.
